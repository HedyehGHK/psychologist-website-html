Services

Therapeutic support tailored to emotional well-being, connection, and growth.

Explore the services offered by Evergreen Therapy, designed to support individuals, couples, and families through life’s emotional and relational challenges.

What We Offer

Thoughtful, client-centered services that help create clarity, resilience, and meaningful progress.

Individual Therapy

Support for anxiety, stress, emotional overwhelm, self-esteem, and personal development in a private one-on-one setting.

Couples Counselling

Guidance for couples working through communication issues, trust concerns, emotional disconnection, and conflict.

Stress & Anxiety Support

Practical therapeutic strategies to help clients manage worry, emotional pressure, tension, and burnout.

01

Individual Therapy

Individual therapy offers a safe and confidential space to explore personal concerns, emotional struggles, and patterns that may be affecting daily life.

The goal is to help clients gain deeper self-understanding, strengthen coping skills, and move toward healthier, more balanced ways of living.

Focus Areas

  • Anxiety and worry
  • Stress management
  • Life transitions
  • Emotional awareness
  • Self-esteem and growth

02

Couples Counselling

Couples counselling helps partners improve communication, understand each other better, and rebuild emotional connection.

Sessions provide a calm and structured environment where both partners can express their concerns and strengthen their relationship.

Focus Areas

  • Communication challenges
  • Conflict resolution
  • Trust rebuilding
  • Emotional connection
  • Relationship support

03

Stress & Anxiety Support

Many clients feel overwhelmed by ongoing stress, racing thoughts, or emotional pressure from daily life.

This service focuses on helping clients develop calming strategies, reduce overwhelm, and build resilience.

Focus Areas

  • Managing overwhelm
  • Reducing emotional pressure
  • Calming strategies
  • Healthy coping skills
  • Resilience building
